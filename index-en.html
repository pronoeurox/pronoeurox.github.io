About Euromillions

Euromillions is a pan-European lottery game launched in February 2004 and drawn every Tuesday and Friday across nine participating countries (Austria, Belgium, France, Ireland, Luxembourg, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland and the United Kingdom). Each draw selects 5 numbers from 1 to 50 and 2 lucky stars from 1 to 12. PronoEuroX is a desktop application that loads the full draw history and exposes it through classic and advanced statistics.

How do I statistically analyze Euromillions draws since 2004?

The standard method is to load the full draw history and compute, for each main number (1–50) and each lucky star (1–12), its absolute frequency, its gap (number of draws since last appearance) and rolling-window frequencies. PronoEuroX includes all three, plus variance, parity balance, sum distribution, consecutive-pair rate, Markov transitions and FFT-based spectrum analysis on the full 1,979-draw sample since February 2004.

How to reduce Euromillions combinations with statistical filters?

Statistical filtering consists in removing combinations whose structure deviates too far from observed norms: extreme sums, too many odd or even numbers, too many consecutive pairs, or over-represented number groups. PronoEuroX includes a combination reducer that lets you select which optimal filters to apply (sum range, parity balance, gap minimums, consecutive-pair limit) and shows how the candidate pool shrinks while keeping every combination that respects the chosen criteria.

How does the Euromillions grid reducer work?

The reducer starts from the full combinatorial space of 5-number combinations out of 50 and applies a chain of statistical filters computed from the 1,979-draw history: sum range (mean 127.5, typical observed spread), parity balance (about 75% of draws contain 2 or 3 even numbers), consecutive-pair rate (around 18% of draws), and per-number minimum gap. The user can enable or disable each filter and adjust the strictness. The output is the subset of combinations that respect all active criteria.
